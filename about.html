About SmartStudyPlanner

Your Smart Study Companion

What is SmartStudyPlanner?

SmartStudyPlanner is a modern web application designed to help students organize their study sessions, manage time effectively, and track academic progress. It combines productivity techniques with intelligent analytics to optimize your learning experience.

Key Features

  • ✓ Pomodoro Timer: 25-minute focused study sessions to improve concentration
  • ✓ Subject Management: Organize your courses and subjects in one place
  • ✓ Session Tracking: Log every study session with duration and notes
  • ✓ Study Calendar: View your permanent study history by date, with completed and pending topics tracked automatically
  • ✓ Analytics Dashboard: View detailed statistics about your study habits
  • ✓ Local Storage: All data is saved locally for privacy and offline access
  • ✓ Responsive Design: Works seamlessly on desktop and mobile devices

How to Use

  1. Add Subjects: Start by adding the subjects or courses you're studying
  2. Log Sessions: Track your study sessions in the Planner section
  3. Use the Timer: Use the Pomodoro timer on the Dashboard for focused study
  4. Review Analytics: Check your Dashboard to see your study statistics
  5. Revisit the Calendar: Browse the Calendar page anytime to see what you studied on any past date, even after clearing your Planner
